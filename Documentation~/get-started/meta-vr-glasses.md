# Get started with Meta VR Glasses

Develop for Meta VR Glasses with OpenXR Meta.

Meta VR Glasses are a pair of lightweight VR glasses tethered to a compute puck. The VR glasses have built-in eye tracking, and eyes and hands are the primary input.

You can develop for Meta VR Glasses with OpenXR Meta 2.6.1 or later, and Unity 6.6 or later.

To learn more, refer to [Develop for Meta VR Glasses](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.6/Documentation/Manual/xr-meta-vr-glasses.html) (Unity Manual).
